# Licensing summary

## USDA FoodData Central

- **Status:** reuse rights are clear for USDA FoodData Central data under CC0 1.0/public-domain dedication.
- **Commercial use, redistribution, and derivatives:** CC0 permits these without attribution conditions.
- **Attribution:** not a CC0 legal condition; retain dataset name, FDC ID, and record URL as a project traceability practice.
- **Limits:** preserve the distinction between Foundation Foods and SR Legacy and the record's exact food description/form. Do not infer a food identity or preparation state not stated by the record.
- Official source: <https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/>; API guide: <https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/api-guide/>.

## UK CoFID 2021

- **Status:** GOV.UK states that its content is available under the Open Government Licence v3.0 except where otherwise stated. The official CoFID publication page and downloadable workbook were used for this evaluation.
- **Commercial use, redistribution, and derivatives:** allowed under OGL v3.0 subject to its conditions.
- **Attribution:** include the source and licence, for example: “Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.” Identify CoFID 2021 and preserve its food-code and main-data-reference citations. Do not imply government endorsement.
- **Third-party data:** OGL does not license rights that belong to third parties. CoFID identifies source references within the workbook; check item-level third-party restrictions where applicable and retain those references.
- Official publication: <https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/composition-of-foods-integrated-dataset-cofid>
- Licence text: <https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/>

## Indian Nutrient Databank raw-food records

- **Status:** unconfirmed. “Open access,” a public download, and the article's CC BY 4.0 licence do not prove the workbook or underlying food-composition data are licensed for reuse.
- **Commercial use, redistribution, derivatives, attribution:** no dataset-level terms covering these uses were found for the raw-food dataset.
- **Required action:** obtain written permission from the INDB owners/maintainers for the exact raw-food file and its incorporated sources, and from ICMR-NIN for electronic product use of IFCT 2017/2004 values. Ask explicitly about commercial use, redistribution, derivatives, attribution, and source-specific restrictions.
- Paper: <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11277795/>
- Repository: <https://github.com/lindsayjaacks/Indian-Nutrient-Databank-INDB->
- Portal: <https://www.anuvaad.org.in/indian-nutrient-databank/>

## Safe handling decision

USDA and CoFID candidates may proceed to a controlled import review under their respective licences, with item-level identity and source checks. Do not use INDB raw-food data until written permissions are obtained. No downloaded source workbook is stored in production application data or added to the project; the CoFID and repository workbooks used here remain in temporary research storage.
